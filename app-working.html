Executive Deep-Dive

CEO Masterclass: Kis Tab Me Kya Karega?

As the apex authority of Gretex Group, Rajesh Sharma (CEO) has complete line-of-sight across all business units (GIL, GCS, GSB, Platinumone, Bahutex). Here is the precise tab-by-tab guide for the CEO.

1. Projects Tab

Strategic Project Creation & Cost Control
  • Create New Projects: Click + New Project to launch major corporate initiatives (e.g. "Turnover ₹80 Cr Expansion").
  • Assign Project Managers: Delegate ownership of projects to Admins, HODs, or Managers.
  • Monitor Derived Cost (₹): Instantly see the actual salary-derived manpower expense burned per project in real-time.
  • Bulk Matrix Allotment: Define multiple KRA nodes and batch-allot tasks to entire teams simultaneously.

2. KRA Builder Tab

Define Group Vision & Cascading Hierarchy
  • Create Main KRAs: Define the top-level strategic drivers (e.g. "GIL — Turnover ₹80 Cr & Profit ₹4 Cr FY27").
  • Cascade Sub-KRAs: Break goals down into Sub-KRAs, Tasks, and Sub-tasks with unlimited depth.
  • Design Mandatory Questions: Define up to 4 variable questions (e.g. Target Reach, Creative Cost) prompted automatically to assignees.
  • Assign Hierarchy Owners: Designate HODs and Managers as accountable owners of specific branches.

3. Dashboards Tab

Executive Cockpit & Live Operations BI
  • Group-wide Manpower Hours: Real-time aggregate of hours worked across all subsidiaries.
  • Cost by Business Unit: View which subsidiary (GIL vs GCS vs GSB) is consuming maximum budget.
  • WIP vs Completed Deliverables: Monitor operational velocity and identify stalled milestones.
  • Rework Rate Metric: Track work quality — if rework rate exceeds 10%, address departmental training.

4. Approvals Tab

Executive Sign-offs & Administrative Override
  • Final Step Approver: Act as the final tier of high-value deliverables (e.g. Brand Campaigns, IPO Filings).
  • Executive Override: As CEO, can view and approve ANY stuck deliverable regardless of which manager's queue it is in.
  • Send for Rework: Direct feedback with comments when a deliverable fails to meet executive standards.
  • Drive File Inspection: Preview attached marketing PDFs, video creatives, and spreadsheets stored in Drive.

5. Org Hierarchy Tree Tab

Span of Control & Chain of Command
  • Company-wide Org Chart: Expand and collapse the full 6-tier reporting tree from CEO down to junior staff.
  • Detect Management Gaps: Identify any employee without a direct reporting superior.
  • Departmental Breakdown: Verify that HODs and Team Leaders are supervising appropriate headcounts.

6. Master Data Tab

Corporate Governance & Salary Oversight
  • Salary & Payroll Visibility: Review confidential employee monthly salaries used in manpower calculation.
  • Department & Location Management: Create new departments with designated HODs, add branch locations, or import them in bulk via CSV.
  • Business Units Management: Add or modify corporate subsidiaries (e.g. GIL, GCS, GSB, Platinumone, Bahutex).
  • Active / Inactive Status Toggles: Safely deactivate obsolete departments, branch locations, or users in 1 click without losing historical task archives.
  • Configure Approval Chains: Define custom multi-step rules for sensitive strategic outputs.
Step-by-Step Tutorial

How to Create a Project in KARYA

Projects tie together business units, designated team members, and bulk task allotting. Available for CEO, Admin, HOD, and Managers.

1

Open Projects Screen

Click on Projects in the left sidebar navigation.

2

Click '+ New Project'

Click the blue button in the top right header next to the project dropdown.

3

Enter Project Details

Fill Name, select Business Unit (GIL, GCS, etc.), pick Manager, and check team members.

4

Save & Allot Tasks

Click Create Project. It automatically saves to Google Sheets DB and is immediately selectable.

Why Projects Matter in KARYA:

1. Derived Cost Tracking: Every task logged under this project contributes its time to the total project budget.

2. Bulk Task Matrix: Once a project is selected, managers can add 10 rows of KRA nodes and batch-allot them to team members in one click.

3. Executive Line-of-Sight: CEO and HODs can monitor how many deliverables are Completed, WIP, or pending approval under that specific initiative.

Core Engine

KRA Hierarchy Builder & Nearest-Ancestor Inheritance

KRAs in Karya can be nested to unlimited depth. Child nodes automatically inherit Variable Fields, Owners, and Approval Chains from parent nodes unless overridden.

How the "Nearest-Ancestor-Wins" Rule Works

🌱 Root: Main KRA (GIL — Turnover ₹80 Cr)

Owner: Rajesh Sharma (CEO) · Approval Flow: Creative Standard Flow

↳ Level 1: Sub-KRA (Marketing & Brand Visibility)

Variable Fields Defined: ["Campaign Theme", "Target Reach", "Channel", "Cost"]

↳ Level 2: Task (Social Media Creatives & Video)

✅ Inherits Variable Questions: Automatically inherits the 4 questions from Level 1!
✅ Inherits Approval Flow: Automatically routes to Creative Standard Flow from Root!

1. Variable Questions Up to 4 custom prompts per node. If left blank, automatically walks up the tree to the nearest parent with questions.
2. Approval Chains Assign approval flow at any node. If not set, inherits from parent or Main KRA.
3. Accountability Owners Assign specific Managers or HODs as owners of nodes to establish accountability.
Distribution Engine

Task Allotment & The Fan-Out Mechanism

In corporate operations, assigning 1 KRA to 5 team members should not take 5 manual entries. KARYA features a high-speed Fan-out Engine.

The Fan-Out Formula

When allocating a task, you can select Multiple Assignees at the same time. The backend automatically creates:

Total Tasks Created = (Selected KRA Nodes) × (Selected Assignees)

Every task created in that batch shares a unique groupId (Batch ID), allowing managers to trace and audit collective output effortlessly.

Three Ways to Allot Tasks in KARYA

  • 1. 'Allot Tasks' Tab: Interactive form with multi-employee fan-out, priority, date, and inherited approval flow.
  • 2. 'Projects' Bulk Matrix: Spreadsheet-like grid to configure 10+ different tasks and assignees at once.
  • 3. 'CSV Import' Tab: Download CSV template, fill rows in Excel, and upload 500 tasks in 5 seconds.
Employee Execution

Daily Reporting, Effort Logging & Self-Log

The My Tasks screen is the home screen for every employee (including HODs and Managers performing individual tasks).

1

Select Log Date & Enter Minutes

Pick any date from the date picker and enter exact minutes worked today (e.g. 120 for 2 hours). KARYA tracks daily effort over time.

2

Answer KRA Variable Fields

If the KRA node has custom questions (e.g. "Platform", "Post URL"), answer them directly in the card before saving.

3

Update Status & Remarks

Set status to Pending, WIP (Work in Progress), Completed, or Hold. If WIP, enter reason in remarks.

Self-Logging Unassigned Tasks (FR-TA-12)

If an employee is asked to perform an ad-hoc emergency task not formally allotted by their manager, they can click '+ Self-log Task' on My Tasks. They choose the Project and KRA, log their initial minutes, and the task is immediately recorded and linked to their supervisor for review.

Task Handover & Reassignment (FR-RPT-07)

If an employee is going on leave or work needs to be shifted, they click the Reassign button, select the colleague, and enter Mandatory Handover Remarks. An immutable audit trail is saved in the task log.

Quality Assurance

Multi-Step Deliverable Approval & Rework Cycle

Deliverables in KARYA (designs, audit sheets, presentations) do not get approved informally. They follow formal, structured Google Drive-backed workflows.

1

Employee Submits Deliverable

Uploads PDF/Image/Video (up to 20MB) directly to Google Drive with remarks.

2

Step 1 Approver (e.g. TL / Manager)

Inspects Drive file. Decides: Approve (advances) or Rework (returns).

3

Step 2 Approver (HOD / CEO)

Final sign-off. Once approved at final step, task state becomes Approved.

What Happens When a Deliverable is Marked "Rework"?
  • The task status changes to approvalState: "rework" with a bright red banner.
  • The approver’s exact correction remarks are displayed prominently on the employee’s task card.
  • The approval step counter resets to Step 0, ensuring the re-done work is vetted again from the start.
  • The rework incident is logged in Dashboards to measure departmental First-Time-Right (FTR) quality.
Financial Intelligence

Derived Manpower Cost Formula & Line-of-Sight

KARYA computes the precise monetary cost of every task, project, and department based on actual employee monthly remuneration.

Official Formula (FR-PRJ-02 / FR-RPT-08)
Cost (₹) = (Minutes Logged / 480) × (Monthly Salary / 30)

Based on a standard 8-hour workday (480 minutes) and 30 calendar days per billing month.

Example 1: Graphic Designer (Rahul Verma)

• Monthly Salary: ₹40,000

• Daily Rate: ₹40,000 / 30 = ₹1,333.33 / day

• Task Effort: 240 minutes (0.5 day)

• Derived Task Cost = 0.5 × ₹1,333.33 = ₹666.67

Confidentiality & Access Line-of-Sight

Salary amounts are strictly confidential. In KARYA:

• 👑 CEO & 🛡️ Admin: Can view individual salaries and total monetary cost.

• 🏢 HOD: Can view aggregate departmental manpower spend.

• 💼 Employees & TLs: See only minutes and hours worked, never salary numbers.
